overview: "KnowledgeHub is a personalized technology intelligence platform — not simply a newsletter. A Python collector worker continuously ingests articles from curated RSS sources and scrapers, enriches them with AI (summaries, topics, keywords, embeddings, quality scores), and stores them in a shared PostgreSQL database. A composite recommendation engine ranks content per user from topic weights, engagement, freshness, and diversity — without relying solely on LLM prompts. Users receive beautifully formatted, tracked weekly editions via email."
